# Journal registry sources

Checked on **2026-10-16** against the [official UT Dallas journal list](https://jsom.utdallas.edu/the-utd-top-100-business-school-research-rankings/index.php). The registry contains those 24 journals, all starting at 2006. This is a journal selection, not a reproduction of UTD's ranking methodology or its article counts.

The original Management Science, Information Systems Research, Marketing Science, and Operations Research rows retain every existing field, including their public IDs and slugs; only the new optional `crossref_issns` column is appended. Names use publisher spelling: UTD's “Journal on Computing” is **INFORMS Journal on Computing**; “Manufacturing and Service Operations Management” is **Manufacturing & Service Operations Management**; “Journal of Finance” is **The Journal of Finance**. Journal of Management Information Systems is not in this list.

Print and electronic ISSNs were checked against the publisher/society pages below and Crossref's journal endpoint. Publisher names are current display names; historical deposits can name earlier publishers. An empty `issn_l` means it has not been independently verified as a linking ISSN; it does not remove either medium's verified ISSN from the registry. Populated new ISSN-L values were confirmed in the ISSN Portal records linked below.

## Provider differences to preserve

- **JAE and JFE electronic endpoints:** `1879-1980` and `1879-2774` are valid electronic ISSNs confirmed by the ISSN Portal, but both their Crossref `/journals/{issn}` and `/journals/{issn}/works` endpoints returned HTTP 404 on the audit date. Their print endpoints work and Crossref's journal metadata lists only print. The registry preserves their real electronic ISSNs and explicitly sets `crossref_issns` to `0165-4101` for JAE and `0304-405X` for JFE. Every other journal leaves this column empty, meaning query all registered ISSNs. A later change to this query coverage requires another complete initial import. A 404 is not treated as an empty journal or silently ignored.
- **Extra identifiers in Crossref journal aggregates:** JOC additionally lists `0899-1499` and `2326-3245`; MSOM lists `1526-5489`; JIBS lists `0047-8210`. These are not added to the registry without publisher evidence. The MSOM extra value fails the ISSN checksum. A read-only scan of every DOI/ISSN pair in the requested period found only the official print/electronic pair for MSOM (1,635 works) and JIBS (1,663), and the official print ISSN for all JOC works (1,921; 1,919 also had its electronic ISSN). No invalid extra ISSN appeared in those work records, so the journal aggregate anomaly alone does not justify relaxing work validation.
- **Medium labels and publisher transfers:** Crossref repeats JM's print ISSN as an electronic identifier; use the independently verified `1547-7185`. POM is now with Sage ([transfer list](https://us.sagepub.com/en-us/nam/new-journals-and-changes)); older Wiley deposits are expected. JOM's current homepage and publisher are Wiley. MISQ stopped printing beginning with volume 44 (2020), but the historic print ISSN remains useful for the 2006-onward catalogue.

No production database was modified as part of this registry audit.
